01 / THE IDEA

A game with an
engineering core.

Physical gestures become digital events. Those events drive light, sound, a moving timer arm, and a treat-dispenser mechanism.

HOW TO PLAY
01

Set the challenge

Turn the potentiometer to select one of five difficulty levels. The required cuts scale from 5 to 25 per cucumber.

02

Grab the handle

A capacitive touch sensor recognizes the player holding the knife. A three-second audio countdown begins.

03

Lift, then chop

Raise the knife to clear the break-beam before pressing the chop button. Valid chops count toward the selected target.

04

Earn the reward

Completed cucumbers earn treats. When the 60-second round ends, a servo-driven dispenser delivers the earned reward.

PLAYABLE DEMO01 / INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE

Your turn to chop.

Turn the difficulty knob, hold the knife handle through the 3-second countdown, then drag it up and down to make each cut. Keep holding until time runs out!

IMPORTANT TRANSITIONS

What happens outside the normal flow?

COUNTDOWN CANCELIf the handle is released during the three-second countdown, stop the countdown and return to difficulty selection.
DEADMANIf the handle is released during play, temporarily reject chops. Re-grabbing within three seconds cancels the timeout; otherwise, abort and reset.
ROUND ENDWhen the 60-second timer expires, stop gameplay, dispense any earned treats, and return to selection after dispensing finishes.

06 / SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE

Code that works
in concert.

The PIC32 runs an Events and Services framework. Choose a service below to see a readable pseudocode breakdown of how it handles events, timers, and output behavior.

SetupService.c

Selects difficulty and starts the game only after a completed countdown.

PSEUDOCODE / LOGIC OVERVIEWNOT COMPILED C
ON INITIALIZE OR RESET:
    stop old timers and active services
    park timer arm
    read potentiometer
    choose difficulty 1 through 5
    set required chops = difficulty × 5
    update LED difficulty display

ON POTENTIOMETER CHANGE (while selecting):
    update difficulty and LED display
    play difficulty feedback sound

ON HANDLE TOUCH (while selecting):
    start 3-second countdown and audio

ON HANDLE RELEASE (during countdown):
    cancel countdown and stop audio
    return to selection

ON COUNTDOWN TIMEOUT:
    start 60-second gameplay timer
    notify PlayService and DeadmanHandling
